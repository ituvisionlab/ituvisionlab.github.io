[New preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33857): _PI-NOMT: Physics-Informed Neural Optimal Mass Transport for Brain Fluid Dynamics_ is now available on arXiv. The work introduces PI-NOMT, a physics-informed framework for estimating hidden tracer transport from dynamic brain MRI.
