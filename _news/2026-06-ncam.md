[New preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22200): _Neural Conjugate Aggregation: Identifiable Unsupervised Multi-Sensor Regression under Heterogeneous Sensor Bias_ is now available on arXiv. The work introduces NCAM, a probabilistic framework for unsupervised multi-sensor fusion with uncertainty quantification and conformal prediction.
